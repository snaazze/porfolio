About Me.

I'm Pranjal — a UI/UX and product designer based in Nashik, India, with a love for end-to-end design: raw user research and journey mapping all the way through to polished interactive prototypes. My work spans event platforms, home services apps, and beyond. I care about the space between user intent and interface response — making that gap as small as possible, and the experience as intuitive as it can be.

Projects.

App

Home Genie

HomeGenie is a mobile app connecting households with verified home service providers — cooks, cleaners, gardeners, and babysitters — on an hourly basis. Designed for two user types, the project focused on building trust, supporting vernacular languages, and creating a fair marketplace where providers set their own rates..

App

FestFlow

This is a college event management platform designed to replace the fragmented stack of WhatsApp, Google Forms, Excel sheets, and paper registers that most student committees rely on. It serves three distinct roles — organisers, committee members, and attendees — each with their own tailored flow covering task ownership, live budget tracking, QR-based gate check-in, and early event discovery. The project spans end-to-end UX: from 8 user interviews and journey maps through to two fully interactive prototypes built across contrasting design systems.
